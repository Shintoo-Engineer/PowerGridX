# PowerGridX ⚡

**Trade Surplus • Power Communities • Smarter Grid**

PowerGridX is a smart digital electricity marketplace that connects **electricity producers, household prosumers, and consumers** through the existing power-grid infrastructure.

The core idea is simple: **if a person or organization generates more electricity than it currently needs, the eligible surplus can be offered through PowerGridX to consumers who need electricity.**

PowerGridX manages the **digital marketplace, matching, forecasting, verification, transactions, contracts, and settlement**, while the existing regulated grid infrastructure handles the physical flow of electricity.

PowerGridX **does not create a dedicated physical electricity connection between an individual seller and a particular buyer**.

# 🏠 The Key Innovation: Household Prosumers

PowerGridX is not limited to large power-generation companies.

A normal person with rooftop solar can participate as a **prosumer**.

### Example

A household has a 5 kW rooftop solar installation.

```text
Solar Generation       10 kWh
Home Consumption        6 kWh
                       ───────
Potential Surplus       4 kWh
```

The user can use the electricity needed by the household and, where permitted, offer eligible surplus through PowerGridX.

```text
        🏠 HOUSE
           │
       ☀️ SOLAR
           │
           ▼
      10 kWh GENERATED
           │
      ┌────┴────┐
      ▼         ▼
   6 kWh       4 kWh
   USED       SURPLUS
                │
                ▼
          POWERGRIDX
                │
                ▼
          MARKETPLACE
```

A prosumer can therefore:

* Generate electricity
* Consume electricity
* Monitor generation
* Calculate surplus
* Offer eligible surplus
* Purchase electricity when required
* Track transactions
* View earnings

# 🔄 How PowerGridX Works

## Step 1 — Register

Users create an account as:

* Prosumer
* Producer
* Consumer

Grid Operator and Admin accounts are restricted.

---

## Step 2 — Add Energy Asset

A prosumer or producer can register an energy-generation asset.

Example:

```text
Asset:
My Rooftop Solar

Source:
Solar

Capacity:
5 kW

Meter:
Verified

Status:
Active
```

---

## Step 3 — Monitor Generation and Consumption

PowerGridX receives meter information or simulated data.

```text
Generation       12.4 kWh
Consumption       7.1 kWh
Potential Surplus 5.3 kWh
```

---

## Step 4 — Offer Surplus

The prosumer selects:

**Sell Surplus Electricity**

and creates an offer.

```text
Source: Rooftop Solar
Available: 3 kWh
Time: 2 PM – 5 PM
Price: ₹X/kWh
Seller: Verified Prosumer
```

---

## Step 5 — Consumer Creates Demand

A consumer can specify:

```text
Required Energy: 300 kWh
Time: 2 PM – 5 PM
Maximum Price: ₹X/kWh
Preferred Source: Renewable
```

---

## Step 6 — Smart Matching

PowerGridX compares:

* Energy quantity
* Price
* Time
* Energy source
* Region
* Eligibility
* Grid/network constraints

and identifies compatible offers.

---

## Step 7 — Transaction

A matched transaction is created.

```text
Offer
  ↓
Match
  ↓
Transaction
  ↓
Meter Verification
  ↓
Settlement
```

## 💡 PowerGridX in one sentence

> **PowerGridX is a smart digital electricity marketplace that connects eligible producers and household prosumers with consumers through the existing power grid, allowing surplus generation—such as excess rooftop solar—to be offered through a digital marketplace with intelligent matching, forecasting, verification, transaction management, and settlement.**
